UIDAI Error Intelligence & Operational Guidance

Technical insights and recommended actions for partner entity engineering teams

1. Citizen Consent Denial Surged to 44.52% of All System Failures

SDJWT_USER_DENIED_CONSENT logged 9,358 failures this week, overwhelmingly driven by Urban Company (5,764 consent cancellations). Citizens cancel the prompt when prompted for Aadhaar offline verification.
Action: Advise Urban Company product teams to add an in-app explanatory screen prior to launching mAadhaar, building trust and highlighting why offline verification is safe.

2. Face Auth Match Score Threshold Failures (Code 731)

5,843 transactions failed due to facial recognition confidence falling below UIDAI's verification threshold (code 731), led by Urban Company (2,666), LIC (992), and Gridlines (633).
Action: Integrate pre-capture face frame illumination prompts, guide users to avoid backlighting, and ensure camera autofocus locks before biometric stream capture.

3. Signature Validation & Token Formatting Errors (17.59%)

SDJWT_SIGN_VALIDATION_FAILED (2,225) and INVALID_JWT (1,473) indicate cryptographic handshake mismatches. Protean logged 321 signature failures, while Urban Company had 747 sign failures and 569 invalid JWTs.
Action: Technical audit of certificate rotation, public key registration with UIDAI, and JWT payload expiry timestamps for Protean and Urban Company.

4. Significant Network Timeout Improvement (-24.76% Share Plunge)

Historically, SDJWT_CALLBACK_NETWORK_ERROR was the single largest error cause in UIDAI OVSE history (30.06% of all failures, 622K occurrences). This week, it dropped dramatically to only 5.30% (1,115 occurrences), representing a major stabilization in backend callback infrastructure.

Strategic Insights on Data Sharing Modalities

Executive takeaways on citizen interaction workflows and entity architecture

1. The Web-to-App Enterprise Expansion (+764% Growth)

Web Intent grew from 0.54% lifetime to 4.67% weekly. Large enterprise portals like Protean (90.7% Web Intent) and LIC (55.7% Web Intent) initiate verification from web portals, triggering mAadhaar on user phones. This is the fastest-growing modality.

2. Kiosk & Assisted Verifications Drive QR Scan

Cross-Device QR represents 2.32% overall, but is vital for physical operations: Procam uses QR for 37.1% (event registrations) and TNMC uses QR for 33.5% (medical council desktop verifications).

3. Consumer Apps Remain 100% App Intent

High-volume mobile consumer apps like Urban Company (21,601 tx) and SelectiveShare (151,735 tx) operate almost exclusively on App Intent (100%), confirming deep native mobile app embedding.
